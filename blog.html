How booking with TableFor works

TableFor exists so you don't have to call around, wait on hold or hope there's a table when you arrive. Here's exactly what happens when you book.

1. Pick a place and a time

Search by area, date and party size on the home page, or browse everything on Explore. When you tap Book a Table, you'll only see dates and times that restaurant is actually taking, and party sizes it can seat.

2. Pay a small booking fee, then get it back

Every booking has a ₱100 fee. It isn't a charge on top of your meal: the full amount comes off your food bill when you dine. The fee is simply what makes a booking reliable for the restaurant, which is why they can hold your table.

3. Add a voucher if you have one

Claim vouchers from the Vouchers section on the home page, then enter the code in the booking form. The form checks the rules for you, like the cuisine, party size or time of day, and tells you straight away if a code doesn't fit that booking.

4. Show your reference when you arrive

You'll get a booking reference and can add the booking to your calendar. Your upcoming bookings also appear at the top of Explore, where you can cancel for free up to two hours before. Inside those two hours, the booking fee isn't refunded, because the restaurant has already held the table for you.

Service charge, VAT and discounts: reading your bill in the Philippines

Restaurant bills in the Philippines often have a few lines that surprise first-time diners. Here's what they usually mean.

VAT is usually already in the menu price

The Philippines charges 12% value-added tax. Most restaurants list prices with VAT included, so you won't see it added on top. The receipt may still show a VAT line, which simply tells you how much of the total was tax.

Service charge is common, but not universal

Many restaurants add a service charge, often around 10%, and by law the full amount goes to the staff. It should be stated on the menu or at the entrance. If a service charge is already on the bill, an extra tip is a kind gesture rather than an expectation.

Senior citizen and PWD discounts

Senior citizens and persons with disability get a 20% discount on their own food, plus VAT exemption, when they present a valid ID. In a group, the discount applies to that person's share of the meal, not the whole table. Have the ID ready when you ask for the bill.

How TableFor fits in

Your ₱100 booking fee is taken off the food bill when you dine, and any voucher you applied is shown on your booking. Everything else on the receipt, including service charge and statutory discounts, is handled by the restaurant as usual.

Sisig 101: the Angeles City classic

Long before it turned up on menus around the country, sisig was an Angeles City story. The word itself is old Kapampangan: it first meant a sour dressing for unripe fruit. The dish most people know today took shape much later.

Where the modern version came from

In 1974, Lucia Cunanan, better known as Aling Lucing, opened a small grill by the old railroad crossing in Angeles. She grilled pig ears and cheeks over charcoal, chopped them fine, then seasoned them with onions, calamansi and chilli. It became so popular that Angeles is now widely called the Sisig Capital of the Philippines.

What goes into it

The classic version is simple: grilled pork head parts, a little liver, onions, calamansi and chilli. The sizzling plate and the raw egg on top came later, and plenty of cooks still argue about whether they belong. Try it both ways and pick a side.

How to eat it

Sisig works as a rice meal or as pulutan with cold drinks, so it's as much a late-night dish as a lunch one. Squeeze the calamansi just before you eat, mix everything through, and eat it while it's still crackling.

How to book a group dinner without the headache

Birthdays, reunions, team dinners: the bigger the group, the harder it is to find a table that fits everyone. These habits make it easier.

Check the party limit first

Every restaurant on TableFor lists the largest group it takes through the site. On Explore, turn on Tables for your search with your real headcount, and anywhere that can't seat you drops out of the list.

Book early, and for a real time

Large tables are limited, so book a few days ahead where you can. Pick a time everyone can actually make. It's easier to cancel for free more than two hours before than to squeeze eleven people onto a table for eight.

Use the group voucher

Groups of eight or more can use BARKADA500 for ₱500 off the bill at any TableFor restaurant. Claim it on the home page, then enter it when you book.

Share the plan early

Send everyone the time, the address and the booking reference as soon as the table is confirmed. Add the booking to your calendar from the confirmation screen so the details are in one place, and ask anyone running late to message the group rather than the restaurant.

Agree on the bill before you order

Big tables go smoother when everyone knows how the bill will be split. Decide up front whether you're splitting evenly or paying for what you order, and remember that many restaurants add a service charge for groups.

Planning a date night in Angeles, step by step

A good date night is mostly planning you don't see. Here's a simple way to get it right.

Book, don't walk in

Friday and Saturday evenings go quickly at smaller dining rooms. Booking means you're not standing at the door hoping for a cancellation.

Tell the restaurant what it's for

In the booking form, choose an occasion such as Anniversary or Date night, and add any requests, like a quiet table or a dietary need. It takes ten seconds and gives the restaurant a chance to make the night feel special.

Add a voucher

Italian is a date-night favourite, and PASTA10 takes 10% off food at Italian and pizza places for tables of two or more.

Plan the timing

Book a little earlier than you think you need. Arriving before the main dinner rush usually means a calmer room, quicker service and more time at the table. If the evening includes a surprise, like a cake or a proposal, mention it in the requests field so the team can plan around it.
