About TableFor

Good tables, found fast and kept for you

TableFor is a restaurant discovery and booking platform for Angeles City, Clark, Mabalacat and San Fernando, Pampanga. We help diners find places worth going to, and help restaurants fill their tables with guests who actually arrive.

We started as a couple of picky eaters from Pampanga who were tired of calling around, waiting on hold and turning up to "sorry, we're full". So we built the thing we wanted: a short list of restaurants we'd send our own families to, real availability, and a booking that holds.

What We Do

One booking, two sides of the table

For diners

  • Browse a curated list of restaurants, with hours, location and party limits up front.
  • See which places can take your date, time and group before you book.
  • Get an instant confirmation and a booking reference you can add to your calendar.
  • Claim vouchers by cuisine and apply them when you book.
  • Link GCash, Maya or your bank once, and cancel for free up to 15 minutes before your table.

For restaurants

  • Receive bookings that are paid for upfront, with a reservation tax you set and share in, so fewer tables sit empty.
  • Set your own hours, party sizes and booking rules.
  • See pre-orders and special requests before guests arrive.
  • Check guests in as seated, completed or no-show from one dashboard.
  • Reach diners who are actively looking for a place to eat tonight.

Our Promises

What you can count on

No hidden charges

Every amount is shown before you pay, with a ? next to each one that explains it. The ₱250-per-guest cancellation fee is never charged if you show up, and the pre-order deposit comes off your bill. The only extra is the reservation tax, which pays for TableFor's service.

No paid rankings

Restaurants can't pay to appear higher. Results are sorted by bookings, rating, distance or price, and you choose which.

Your details stay yours

Your name and number go only to the restaurant you book, and only for that booking.

Booking Policies

Clear rules for every booking

The same rules apply at every TableFor restaurant, so you always know where you stand.

Link a payment method, pay to reserve

When you create your account, you link GCash, Maya or a bank account. To reserve, you pay the reservation tax and 20% of any dishes you pre-order. The ₱250-per-guest cancellation fee isn't paid now: it stays on hold on your linked account.

Reservation tax

Pays for TableFor's booking service: finding and holding your table, and handling your payment and booking. Each restaurant sets its own amount, from ₱100, and receives a share of it. It isn't taken off your bill.

Cancelling your booking

15+ minutes before: free. You get your payment back, the cancellation fee is never charged, and any voucher you used is freed up.

Under 15 minutes, after it starts, or a no-show: your cancellation fee is charged to your linked account and paid to the restaurant, along with any pre-order deposit. The reservation tax isn't refunded. We'll warn you before you confirm.

15-minute grace period

Your table is held for 15 minutes after your booking time. After that the restaurant may release it, and the booking counts as a no-show.

Restaurant cancellations

If a restaurant declines or cancels your booking, you get your full payment back and a note in My bookings.

Groups and vouchers

Book up to 20 guests, or the restaurant's own limit. One voucher per booking, and each voucher works once.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Is TableFor free to use?

Browsing is free. When you create an account, you link GCash, Maya or a bank account. To reserve, you pay the reservation tax (set by the restaurant, from ₱100) and 20% of any pre-order, which comes off your bill. The reservation tax pays for TableFor's booking service, and the restaurant receives a share of it. The ₱250-per-guest cancellation fee is only charged if you cancel late or don't show up.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Open My bookings from your profile icon, or your upcoming bookings on Explore. Cancel at least 15 minutes before and your payment is refunded in full, and you're never charged the cancellation fee. After that, or if you don't turn up, your ₱250-per-guest cancellation fee is charged to your linked account and paid to the restaurant, any pre-order deposit goes to the restaurant, and the reservation tax isn't refunded. If free cancellation is over, we'll show you exactly what you'll be charged and ask you to confirm first. See our booking policies.

What if I'm running late?

Your table is held for 15 minutes after your booking time. After that the restaurant may give it to other guests, and the booking counts as a no-show. If you're running late, call the restaurant from your booking details.

What if the restaurant cancels?

If a restaurant declines or cancels your booking, your payment is refunded in full, no cancellation fee is charged, and you'll see the update in My bookings.

How do vouchers work?

Claim a voucher in the Vouchers section on the home page, then enter its code in the booking form. Each voucher lists the cuisines, party size or times it works for, and the form tells you straight away if a code doesn't fit.

How big a group can I book?

Up to 20 guests per booking, depending on the restaurant. Each restaurant shows its own limit. For larger events, contact the restaurant directly.

Where do ratings come from?

From diners who booked through TableFor and completed their visit. You can only leave a review after your meal.

I own a restaurant. How do I join?

Apply on our Partner page. We'll visit, try the food and walk you through the Partner Portal before your listing goes live.
