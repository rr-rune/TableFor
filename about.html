About TableFor

Good tables, found fast and kept for you

TableFor is a restaurant discovery and booking platform for Angeles City and Clark. We help diners find places worth going to, and help restaurants fill their tables with guests who actually arrive.

We started as a couple of picky eaters from Pampanga who were tired of calling around, waiting on hold and turning up to "sorry, we're full". So we built the thing we wanted: a short list of restaurants we'd send our own families to, real availability, and a booking that holds.

What We Do

One booking, two sides of the table

For diners

  • Browse a curated list of restaurants, with hours, location and party limits up front.
  • See which places can take your date, time and group before you book.
  • Get an instant confirmation and a booking reference you can add to your calendar.
  • Claim vouchers by cuisine and apply them when you book.
  • Cancel for free up to two hours before your table.

For restaurants

  • Receive confirmed bookings backed by a small fee, so fewer tables sit empty.
  • Set your own hours, party sizes and booking rules.
  • See pre-orders and special requests before guests arrive.
  • Check guests in as seated, completed or no-show from one dashboard.
  • Reach diners who are actively looking for a place to eat tonight.

How We Choose

Every restaurant earns its place

We would rather list fewer places we trust than every place in town. Before a restaurant joins, we look for four things.

01

Food locals return for

Consistent cooking that regulars recommend, not just a busy launch week.

02

Honest pricing

Menu prices that match what arrives on the bill, with any service charge made clear.

03

Bookings that hold

A team that honours reservations and keeps the table ready for the time you chose.

04

Real reviews

Ratings come from diners who booked and visited, so every review is tied to a real meal.

Our Promises

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Is TableFor free to use?

Browsing is free. Each booking has a ₱100 fee, which is taken off your food bill when you dine, so a completed booking costs you nothing extra.

What happens if I need to cancel?

Cancel from your upcoming bookings on Explore. It's free up to two hours before your table. Inside two hours, the booking fee isn't refunded because the restaurant has already held the table for you.

How do vouchers work?

Claim a voucher in the Vouchers section on the home page, then enter its code in the booking form. Each voucher lists the cuisines, party size or times it works for, and the form tells you straight away if a code doesn't fit.

How big a group can I book?

Up to 20 guests per booking, depending on the restaurant. Each restaurant shows its own limit. For larger events, contact the restaurant directly.

Where do ratings come from?

From diners who booked through TableFor and completed their visit. You can only leave a review after your meal.

I own a restaurant. How do I join?

Apply on our Partner page. We'll visit, try the food and walk you through the Partner Portal before your listing goes live.
